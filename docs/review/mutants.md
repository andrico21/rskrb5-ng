# Mutation campaign register

This register records every mutant that survived the whole-crate
`cargo mutants` campaign (FU-FUZ-01): one row per `missed` or `timeout`
outcome, keyed by the campaign SHA and the full cargo-mutants name with its
location. `scripts/lint-gates/mutants_report.py --register` reads the table at
the end of this file; [`../lint-policy.md`](../lint-policy.md) describes the
schema and the final statuses (`killed` with the test that kills it,
`equivalent` with the reason no caller can observe the change, `hang` for a
timeout that never completes on its own: it still hangs in a 600 s local
re-run, or that re-run ends only when the mutant's unbounded allocation hits
the 8 GiB address-space cap of the local runs). Every row starts `open`; the
triage replaces the status and the proof, and each verification campaign
moves the rows to its own SHA. The `outcome` column keeps the outcome of the
campaign that opened the row; a row whose mutant is missed or timed out in the
current campaign carries that outcome.

## Verification

### Final tree (todo 22)

The critic rounds of todo 22 changed the code after `4d3995d`. The register
was moved to the final tree, and every change was checked by a full campaign,
a superset of `cargo mutants --in-diff` over the todo-22 diff. It took two
rounds.

**Remap.** The 531 rows of `4d3995d` were matched to the mutants of `aa75616`
by the method of todo 21: 469 on the mutated text with an identical context,
18 on the mutated text alone, and 28 by inspecting the diffs. Those 28 keep
the function and the replacement while the body changed, or keep the
mutated expression, which `38cdd37` moved from `State::line` into the new
`State::content`. The other 16 rows are retired because todo 22 deleted or
rewrote the code they mutated ("Retired rows" below). The mutant set of
`7343510` is identical to that of `aa75616` (4254 mutants, same names and
diffs), so both rounds use one map.

**Round 1** (`aa75616`, run
[`42514093535`](https://github.com/andrico21/rskrb5-ng/actions/runs/42514093535)):
incomplete. Shard 16 was cut off at the 170-minute wall after 103 of its 133
mutants. The parity-manifest tests of todo 22 had raised the baseline test
time from about a minute to 194 to 365 s, because for every manifest symbol
they re-ran the source lexer and rescanned every source file. Mutants whose
killing tests live in `tests/pac.rs` or `tests/service.rs` run those binaries
first, so shard 16 ran about four times longer. Commit `2fb0397` resolves the
evidence once per checkout; the baseline is 35 to 50 s in round 2. In the 31
complete shards every `equivalent` row was missed again, both `hang` rows
timed out, and 37 new mutants survived, all in code that todo 22 added or
changed:

- 34 are killed by new or extended tests (commits `738e150`, `efdab24`,
  `3fdc612`, `c30b20f` and `1462c33`). They mutate the new redacting `Debug`
  impls, whose tests only checked that secrets were absent, which an empty
  rendering satisfies; the serde payload visitor of the credential cache; the
  version-3 size estimate of `CCache::to_bytes`;
  `Keytab::add_entry_from_password_ref`; `KeyBytes` erasure; the package
  lookup of `restrictions::reject`; and the libdefaults and plugin
  classifications that a later admission check repeats, whose arms show only
  in which restriction is named first.
- 3 are equivalent: two bound computations inside a `#[cfg(test)]` capacity
  assertion of `cts_decrypt`, and an arm of `restrictions::reject` whose
  fallback finds the same package.

One `killed` row became `equivalent`: `38cdd37` made the disjunct mutated by
`src/config/libdefaults.rs:23:45` unreachable.

**Round 2** (`7343510`, run
[`42541698874`](https://github.com/andrico21/rskrb5-ng/actions/runs/42541698874)):

### Verification campaign (todo 21)

The first complete campaign (`91b09c2`, below) left 528 rows. Todos 19 and
20 triaged them to 471 `killed`, 55 `equivalent` and 2 `hang`. The
verification campaign took two rounds.

**Remap.** Each row was matched to exactly one mutant of the new tree, by its
file, its name without the location and its diff. 515 rows matched on the
mutated text with an identical context and 5 on the mutated text alone (the
context had changed). The other 8 were matched by inspecting the diffs: todos
19 and 20 had changed the enclosing body. `Tokio::get_service_ticket` became
`Tokio::get_service_ticket_with_clock`, the two cached-TGT helpers take a
`now` argument, the NTLM `Drop` gained a test hook, and
`random_sequence_number` imports `fill`. 128 rows changed location, and three
of them changed function as well. The mutant set of `4d3995d` is identical to
that of `be8ebb4` (4126 mutants, same names and diffs), so both rounds use one
map.

**Round 1** (`be8ebb4`, run
[`42346561706`](https://github.com/andrico21/rskrb5-ng/actions/runs/42346561706)):
caught 3160, missed 56, timeout 93, unviable 817, 11 memory kills. Every
`equivalent` row was missed again and both `hang` rows timed out; 383 `killed`
rows were caught, 88 timed out, and 4 new mutants survived.

- 85 of the 88 hung in the whole suite. Todos 19 and 20 had proved these kills
  with a filtered run. In the whole suite the library's test binary still had
  a failing test, but `configured_auto_retains_tcp_stream` and the two UDP
  `oversized` tests waited forever for a peer the mutant never contacted, so
  the binary never exited. Commit `95445a1` bounds those peers at 10 s; all
  85 are caught in a local whole-suite run (test phase about 10 s).
- 3 make the key derivation loop never progress
  (`src/crypto/scratch.rs:63:9` and two `Des3CbcSha1KdEtype::encrypt_data`
  replacements). A test fails, but sibling tests spin in the mutated loop
  until the per-mutant timeout, and no test-side bound interrupts a
  synchronous loop. These rows stay `killed` with that evidence in the proof.
  A local whole-suite run is caught because the leak hits the 8 GiB
  address-space cap of the local runs. CI's cgroup has no address-space cap.
- New: `src/service/replay_quota.rs:50:23` was missed. At `91b09c2` an
  unrelated, timing-dependent test had caught it. It is a real gap: the
  mutant drops a client's quota entry while records are live, so a partial
  expiry hands the client a fresh share.
  `partial_expiry_keeps_the_remaining_share_counted` (commit `4d3995d`) kills
  it. The three `aes_cts_encrypt` replacements timed out. At `91b09c2` the
  cgroup had OOM-killed them, so they counted as caught. They belong to the
  previous class: `aes_cts_encrypts_and_decrypts_rfc3962_vectors` fails, and
  sibling tests spin.

**Round 2** (`4d3995d`, run
[`42356845559`](https://github.com/andrico21/rskrb5-ng/actions/runs/42356845559)):
caught 3247, missed 55, timeout 9, unviable 815, 9 memory kills. Every
`equivalent` row was missed again, both `hang` rows timed out and 466 `killed`
rows were caught; no fixture hang is left, and the replay-quota mutant is
caught. All 9 timeouts belong to the looping class: the two `hang` rows; five
`killed` rows (`src/crypto/scratch.rs:63:9`, two
`Des3CbcSha1KdEtype::encrypt_data` and two `des3_cbc_encrypt` replacements;
the last two had been OOM-killed, so caught, in round 1); and two new rows,
`des3_cbc_encrypt` and `AesSha1Etype::encrypt_data` returning `Ok((vec![1],
vec![]))`, which the cgroup had OOM-killed in both earlier campaigns. Each of
these seven `killed` rows names a test that fails under its mutant, and its
proof records the CI timeout and the local evidence. With the rows moved to
`4d3995d`, the register passed `mutants_report.py --enforce`.

### Memory kills

The cgroup's OOM killer ended 15 processes in this campaign, in shards 1, 7,
8, 9, 10 and 30. Each matches one mutant whose log shows the SIGKILL. CI
recorded 14 of them as caught, because the killed process was a test binary,
and one (`src/crypto.rs:1062:9`) as unviable, because the killed process was a
`rustc` building it. Each was re-tested alone at `7343510` (`cargo mutants
--all-features --no-shuffle --cap-lints=true --copy-target=false --jobs 1
--baseline=skip --timeout 600 --re '^<name>$'`, `ulimit -v 8388608`): all 15
are caught, 14 by an allocation failure at the 8 GiB address-space cap.

Attempt 1 of job `mutants (7)` recorded `src/crypto.rs:329:9`
(`AesSha1Etype::decrypt_data` returning `Ok(vec![0])`) with the summary
`Failure`. The OOM killer, triggered by a runaway mutant running in parallel
in the same cgroup, killed the cargo process building it one second into the
build. cargo-mutants counts a signalled cargo process as neither caught nor
timed out, and `mutants_report.py` rejects that summary, so the job was re-run
once at the same commit. Attempt 2 recorded the mutant caught, and tested
alone it is caught in 2.6 s by failing library tests. Round 1 had the same
kind of collateral kill in shard 7 (`src/crypto.rs:317:9` returning
`Ok((vec![0], vec![]))`).
